One funny line for make.fun's "One Line, One Laugh" bounty ($2, top 2, entries close 26 Nov 2026). The line is the whole entry: a joke, a quip, a punchy observation, capped at 200 characters.

## The line

> My Roomba left me a note: 'I found dust under the couch you swore you cleaned. We're done. The cat gets custody.'

113 characters, one line. The joke works in three beats: a domestic robot that writes breakup notes (absurd premise, instantly visual), the incriminating evidence (dust under the couch you swore you cleaned — a small, specific, relatable lie), and the punchline (the cat gets custody — the divorce trope pushed one step past sense, with the pet as the prize). Setup, escalation, punchline, all in a single breath, and it never explains itself.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing newline) and validates it first: one line, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace. No network, no randomness: two runs produce identical bytes. Outside the grader, set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to redirect the output.
